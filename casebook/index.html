Gamma glossary
Call wall
Strike with the most call-side gamma. Dealers are long gamma there, so their hedging leans against moves near it. A reaction level, not resistance.
Put wall
Strike with the most negative put-side gamma. Dealer hedging sells into declines near it. A reaction level, not support.
Gamma flip (zero gamma)
Price where total dealer gamma crosses zero. Above it moves get damped; below it moves get amplified.
Positive gamma
Spot above the flip. Dealers buy dips and sell rips: choppy, mean-reverting, tighter ranges.
Negative gamma
Spot below the flip. Dealer hedging chases price: trending moves, wider ranges.
Vector flip
Term from the MU/SNDK poster. Not a standard term. Possibly their name for crossing the gamma flip; unconfirmed.
